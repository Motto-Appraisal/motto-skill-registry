## 12. Lessons learned (<subject-address>, Carrollton run, Oct 2026)
- ORDER OF OPERATIONS IN RECONCILIATION: do the subject's porch/features FIRST. Subject Porch/Patio/Deck must reflect reality (this subject had a COVERED patio = Pc/CvPat; I had it as Pc/Pat and every covered-patio adjustment downstream was wrong). Before Feature Adjustments, check the subject's patio/deck/fireplace/carport/shed against photos or the Realist/MLS data, not only the grid default.
- COVERED PATIO = solid roof. A pergola or open slats, or only a roof overhang near the doors, is NOT a covered patio. Comps listed Pc/CvPat in the MLS that show open patios/pergolas in photos should be re-labelled Pc/Pat in the grid and then carry a positive adjustment when the subject has the covered patio. Sign rule: comp lacks what subject has = positive; comp has what subject lacks = negative; same = 0 (type 0 explicitly, no adjustment left over).
- Adjustment cells with a stale value after the subject changes: re-enter 0 on comps that now match the subject (e.g. subject became CvPat, so comps with CvPat went from -3,800 to 0).
- TIME ADJUSTMENTS: the "zero all time adjustments for sales within 6 months" rule is measured against TODAY, per comp. Only zero when the close date is <= 6 months old. Sales older than 6 months (here comps closed 11/25 to 03/26 viewed in Oct 2026) KEEP the model's time adjustment. I wrongly zeroed all of them and had to restore (+1,200 / -500 / +800 / -600). Check each comp's close date before zeroing; also keep Concessions zeroed as before.
- TOTAL ROOMS: the Total Rooms row has an adjustment cell that is blank by default. User preference: where a comp's total rooms differs from the subject, type an explicit 0 in that adjustment cell (done for 8-room and 6-room comps vs the 7-room subject).
- PHOTO CHECK method that works: open viewer by clicking a comp photo, scroll the right photo panel in 5-tick steps with region screenshots [920,160,1260,690], Down arrow to go to the next comp, Esc to leave. Report: fireplace, covered vs open patio, garage/carport/shed. Mismatches to flag, not edit, until the user decides.
- ADDING A COMP LATE: TrueTracts Comp Finder cannot add comps beyond about 1 mile. Instead go to Grid Cleanup and click "Import Additional Comps with Spark" (top bar). It opens a Spark tab; Add Comparables > Upload a single-comp Spark CSV; Next; Export > Go; close the OLD TrueTracts tab; "Open Updated Session". Existing comps and ALL grid edits are preserved; the new comp is appended as the last comp (then clean it: Quality Q4+ -> Q4, fill blank Total Rooms from a similar-GLA comp (flag it), porch to Pc/Pat or Pc/CvPat, add carport/patio adjustments in Reconciliation). The user can drag the new comp to the correct column position themselves.
- MARKING ONE COMP IN MATRIX: type the MLS# into the shorthand box at the top of Matrix to get that single listing, check the row, Export > Spark. The Matrix session expires when idle (marks are lost): `sign_in` on the clareity page, then navigate to the Detailed search again.
- Spark: new Spark tabs opened from TrueTracts reuse the logged-in session; no Subject entry is needed in "additional comps" mode.
- RATING a comp 0 to preview a swap: set the weak comp's rating to 0 in Reconciliation to see the range without it; quality concerns on an ARV comp (e.g. mid-quality materials) can be tested this way before touching the grid.
- Weights: the tool rebalances automatically; comp ratings may change on their own when an adjustment (e.g. time) is zeroed.
- Rating dropdown: click the rating field, then the list shows 10 down to 0; the "0" option is ~381px below the field.
- VIEWPORT FLIPS (1280x720 / 874x816 / 1015x816) are constant. Always re-screenshot after "viewport changed" and re-aim; do batches of 1-2 edits then verify.
- Editing the SUBJECT's grid cell triggers a ~20s recalculation; wait before the next edit. After a cell edit, focus often moves to the adjacent adjustment cell: if so just type the next value and Enter.
- Export: preview ready after ~40s; check "MLS Comp Photos"; Export gives a dialog with "Work File Zip" and "Total TDCX File". Click both. Downloads do not reach the sandbox (user's local Downloads); ask the user to confirm and attach if filing to OneDrive is needed.
- Cost approach: raising quality to reach the ARV after adding the porch ended at Q5.5/age 6 for a $475k ARV on this 1974 house; the Q4 start is only a starting point.

- CARPORT PHOTO VERIFICATION (user correction 2026-10-17, 1713 Baylor comp 1 1203 Edgewood: MLS/Spark said carport, photos showed none, and I adjusted -3,900 on a baseless claim): a carport is NEVER trusted from MLS/Spark data alone. For EVERY comp (and the subject) where the grid Carport count is >0, or the MLS text mentions carport, open ALL photos (viewer in section 12 / Matrix photos) and confirm a roofed open-sided structure actually exists. Treat it like any other amenity: if photos do not show it, set the grid Carport count to 0, type NO carport adjustment (explicit 0), and list it in the report as 'carport removed, not in photos'. If a garage is shown and the MLS 'carport' is just the attached garage/porte-cochere/driveway cover, remove it too. Also check comps whose Spark Carport is 0 but photos show one. Do this in Grid Cleanup BEFORE Feature Adjustments/Reconciliation so no carport adjustment is typed unverified. If photos are inconclusive, flag it instead of adjusting.
- CARPORT (learned <subject-address>, re-do): the Carport feature adjustment ($<amount> default) is PER CARPORT STRUCTURE, not per space. A 2-space carport is ONE carport = -3,900 once (never -7,800). Set the grid Carport count to 1 for a single (even 2-car) carport. MLS "Carport Spaces = 2" is a space count, so convert to 1 structure.
